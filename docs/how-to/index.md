---
title: How-to guides
description: Recipes for tasks you already have in mind, each one task from start to finish, from setting aless up to a grammar of your own.
---
Each guide solves one task. It assumes aless is installed and that you know your way around a terminal, and it links to the [command line reference](/reference/command-line.html) for every option it uses and to the [keys](/reference/keys.html) for every key.

The guides are in two groups. The first is setting aless up: installing it, its completions and its manual page, and what each platform needs of a terminal. The second is about a file open in the viewer: searching it, following it as it changes, copying from it, several files at once, a directory, the format you name, and a format aless does not know, read with a grammar of your own. If you have not used aless before, the [tutorial](/tutorials/index.html) comes first.
