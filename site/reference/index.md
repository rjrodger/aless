The first two pages are written from the aless binary when this site is built, from the tables `aless --help`, the man page and the F1 help are printed from, and the third from the repository's changelog, so they say what aless {{version}} says of itself. A page's Markdown twin, at its address with `.md` for `.html`, and the plain text the terminal shows, with `.txt`, are written at the same time.
